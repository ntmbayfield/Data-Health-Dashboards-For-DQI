The em dash

CodeMeaning
—A design note with no decision or requirement attached — an explanation of why the board is built the way it is, or a caution about how a number should be read. Nobody has to sign anything, but ignoring one of these is usually how a board stops being believed. Examples: why five never-attested rows should not be backfilled; why duplicate-rule blocks are saves rather than failures; why every alert must open a worklist.
